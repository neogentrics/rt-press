# Getting help

**Something's wrong.** [Report a bug](https://github.com/neogentrics/rt-press/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include your
version (it's in *Help → About*) and the steps that cause it. A screenshot helps, but please don't include your unpublished
manuscript, an ISBN list or a login.

**You have an idea.** [Suggest a feature](https://github.com/neogentrics/rt-press/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). Check the
[roadmap](ROADMAP.md) first, since it may already be planned.

**It's a security problem.** Please don't open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

RT Press is free, and support is on a best-effort basis.
