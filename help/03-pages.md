# The book's pages

[Pages and Their Order…](#do=book-pages) lists everything in your book, in the order it is set: front matter, parts, chapters and back matter.

## Adding pages

Under **Add a page**, choose what you want: a title page, copyright, dedication, acknowledgements, contents, introduction, a note to the reader, a prologue, a part, an epilogue, an appendix, a preview of the next book, also by, stay connected, or a page of your own. It goes where such a page usually stands. **Up** and **Down** move it.

- A **title page** and a **contents page** are filled in by RT Press. The title page has the book's title, subtitle and author.
- A **copyright page** comes with words to start from. Read them and make them yours: they aren't legal advice.
- A **dedication**, a **copyright page** and a **title page** carry no page number unless you ask.
- A **prologue** and an **epilogue** are set as chapters are, without a chapter number.
- A new **part** goes after your story as it stands. Move it up to where it begins.

## Writing a page

**Edit** opens a page's title, subtitle and words:

- A blank line ends a paragraph.
- `*italic*`, `**bold**` and `***both***` go between stars.
- A line with only `* * *` is a scene break.
- A line that starts with `>` belongs to a letter, a log or a sign.
- A line that ends in `\` keeps its line break.
- `{.Subheading}`, `{.Centred}` or `{.Listed}` at the start of a paragraph makes it a subheading, centres it, or sets it as a line of a list. Books brought in from files use these. A name that isn't one of them is set as ordinary text, and the build says so.

A page you don't change is left exactly as it was.

## Which pages you can change

In a book made from files, you can correct, move and remove anything, and add chapters. Your files themselves are only read. A book from RT Story Tracker keeps its words and the order of its chapters: change those there. The pages you add here are yours, and you can put them anywhere among the chapters.

When a book is brought in again, the pages you added go back after the pages they followed.
