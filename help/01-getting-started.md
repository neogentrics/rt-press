# Getting started

RT Press lays out a finished book for print, checks it against your printer's rules, and saves a PDF only when every check passes. Your words never leave your computer.

**The fastest way to learn the app is the [tutorial](#do=tutorial).** It takes a few minutes and uses a practice book.

## From chapters to a PDF

1. [Import your chapters](#do=import-chapters): a folder of Word files, one for each chapter.
2. Give the book its title and author, and choose the interior: the edition, trim size, paper and type.
3. **Build the Interior**. RT Press sets the pages, repairs page turns, and checks the file.
4. Look through the pages, then **Save PDF…**.

## Finding your way around

- **The toolbar** has the things you use most: import, build, save, Go to, the theme button, Preferences and help. The theme button keeps the window light or dark, or has it follow your system (*Auto*).
- **Go to** (`Ctrl+K`, or `Cmd+K` on a Mac) jumps to a chapter. Type `>` there to run any command by name.
- **The menus** hold commands. Every setting for the app is in [Preferences](#do=preferences).
