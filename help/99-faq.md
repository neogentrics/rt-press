# Common questions

## Why can't I save my book?

A check failed. Open **The checks** to see which. For a very short book it is usually the page count: see [the checks](#help=checks).

## Will RT Press change my words?

No. It reads your files and sets them. A page-turn repair only moves where a sentence starts.

## Can I use my own fonts?

Yes. Choose a body font, a title font and a subtitle font under **Interior**: those that come with RT Press, or one on your computer that allows being put in a PDF. See [Setting the interior](#help=interior).

## How do I add a dedication, or move a page?

Use **Pages and their order…**, beside your chapters or under **File**. See [the book's pages](#help=pages).

## Does RT Press make the cover?

Not yet. The cover wrap, with the spine width for your page count, is planned.

## Where is the PDF?

Wherever you chose when you used **Save PDF…**. See [saving](#help=saving).

## How do I take the tour again?

Choose [Help → Tutorial](#do=tutorial) at any time.
