# Setting the interior

These settings are under **Interior**, beside your chapters.

## The book you're printing

- **Edition**: paperback or hardcover.
- **Trim size**: the size of the finished page. The sizes KDP prints are listed. A paperback can also take **Custom size…**, 4 to 8.5 in wide and 6 to 11.69 in tall. A size wider than 6.12 in or taller than 9 in costs more to print.
- **Paper and ink**: only the papers KDP offers for that trim are listed, with their page limits. KDP publishes no page limits for a custom size, so RT Press holds one to the limits of the smallest listed size that is at least as wide and as tall, and says so. KDP's previewer has the last word.
- **Bleed**: turn it on if anything in your book reaches the edge of the page. RT Press adds 0.125 in on the top, bottom and outside edges, and none at the spine.

## The book theme

A **book theme** is the design of your printed pages. It has nothing to do with the window's colours. Choose one under **Book theme**:

- **Classic**: Garamond, centred titles in capitals and a drop cap. No running heads.
- **Inscription**: a Palatino-style text under carved capitals, with "Chapter One" and a diamond between scenes.
- **Literary**: Baskerville with display titles and italic running heads.
- **Modern**: a clear serif under sans titles on the left, numbered chapters and the chapter in the running head.
- **Plain**: one quiet serif, no drop cap and no running heads.

Choosing a theme fills in the settings below. Change any of them, then use **Save as my theme…** to keep the design under a name of your own, for any book. Changing or deleting a theme never changes a book you have already set.

Once pages are showing, a change shows in them in a moment. That quick preview doesn't mend page turns or check the file: **Build the Interior** does.

## The pages

- **Body font**, **title font** and **subtitle font** can be any that comes with RT Press, or one on your computer that allows being put in a PDF. The large first letter of a chapter goes with the titles. Ornaments are set in Noto Sans Symbols 2.
- If a book asks for Book Antiqua, Felix Titling or Segoe UI Symbol and your computer hasn't it, or it can't be put in a PDF, RT Press sets the book in an open stand-in (TeX Gyre Pagella, Cinzel or Noto Sans Symbols 2) and says so after the build.
- If a font lacks a character or a style your book uses, the build stops and names it and where it is. Nothing is taken from another font.
- **Body text** and **line spacing** set the size of the type.
- **Page numbers** go on the outside edge or in the middle, with dashes round them if you like.
- **Scene break** is what stands between two scenes.
- **Drop cap**, **title page** and **contents page** can each be turned on or off.
- **Chapter openers and running heads** holds what stands above a chapter's title (nothing, its number, Roman numerals, "Chapter 1" or "Chapter One"; numbers in words are English only), where titles stand and how big they are, the space round them, a mark under them, and the **running heads**: your name on left-hand pages and the book's title or the chapter's on right-hand pages. No head is printed on a chapter's first page, a part's page or a blank page.
- A chapter's heading, a scene break and a document inside the story each take a whole number of lines, so full pages end on the same line.
- **Inside margin** is the one at the spine. Left empty, it comes from the page count: what KDP asks the binding of that many pages to have, and 0.375 in more. A book of 151 to 300 pages gets 0.875 in. You can set your own; a build then fails its check if it is narrower than KDP allows. The other margins are 0.625 in outside, 0.8 in at the top and 0.9 in at the bottom.
- **Pin the page count** keeps a published page count, so an existing cover still fits. RT Press adds blank pages to reach it, and says so if the book is too long.

## House rules

Each is its own switch, and all are on to begin with:

- **Every title starts on a right-hand page**, with a blank page before it when needed.
- **End on a blank page, with an even page count.**
- **Page numbers on blank pages.**
- **A dash never starts a line.** Only the setting changes; your words don't.
- **Break words at line ends (hyphenation)**, by the book's language.

## The book's language

**Book language**, beside the title, sets how words break at line ends, which quotation marks your straight quotes become (for example “ ” in English, „ “ in German and « » in French), and the words RT Press prints itself, such as "Chapter" and the contents page's heading. Quotes you typed as curly marks are left as you typed them.
