# Saving the PDF

**Save PDF…** writes the file only when every check passes. Choose where it goes.

The saved file is ready to upload: its bleed is on the top, bottom and outside edges only, its page boxes say where the trim is, and the links and bookmarks a printer refuses are gone. Its title and author are written into it.

RT Press writes the file. You upload it yourself: RT Press never signs in to a store.

Change a setting or your chapters, and build again before you save.
