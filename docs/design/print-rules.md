# Print rules

The rules RT Press checks a book against. KDP's are the printer's rules and can't be switched off. The house rules are a
preset any author can turn on, change or leave off.

Every KDP number below was read from KDP's own help pages on 6 November 2026. They're read again before each release.

**Read again on 27 November 2026,** for custom sizes: *Set Trim Size, Bleed, and Margins*, *Print Options*, the *Paperback
Submission Guidelines*, *Paperback Printing Cost* and *Create a Paperback Cover*. The custom size range, what makes a trim
large and the inside margin table were unchanged. **None of the five gives page limits for a custom size, or a way to work
them out.** The submission guidelines say only that the most pages depend on ink, paper and trim size.

## KDP: trim and page count

Source: [Set trim size, bleed and margins](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVBQ3CMEQW3W2VL6) and
[Print options](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834180).

**Paperback**, pages from minimum to maximum:

| Trim (inches) | Black, white | Black, cream | Black, groundwood | Standard colour | Premium colour |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 × 8, 5.06 × 7.81, 5.25 × 8, 5.5 × 8.5, 6 × 9 | 24–828 | 24–776 | 24–812 | 72–600 | 24–828 |
| 6.14 × 9.21, 6.69 × 9.61, 7 × 10, 7.44 × 9.69, 7.5 × 9.25, 8 × 10 | 24–828 | 24–776 | 24–812 | 72–600 | 24–828 |
| 8.25 × 6, 8.25 × 8.25 | 24–800 | 24–750 | 24–784 | 72–600 | 24–800 |
| 8.5 × 8.5, 8.5 × 11 | 24–590 | 24–550 | 24–578 | 72–600 | 24–590 |
| 8.27 × 11.69 | 24–780 | 24–730 | 24–764 | not offered | 24–590 |

Custom paperback trim: 4 to 8.5 inches wide, 6 to 11.69 inches tall. Wider than 6.12 or taller than 9 inches is a large
trim and costs more to print.

**Press's own rule for a custom size's page limits, since KDP publishes none:** those of the smallest listed paperback size
(by area) that is at least as wide and as tall. Where several are that small, or none is large enough, the strictest of them.
A paper that size isn't sold in isn't offered. Press says these are its own wherever it uses them, and KDP's previewer has the
last word.

**Hardcover**: 5.5 × 8.5, 6 × 9, 6.14 × 9.21, 7 × 10 and 8.25 × 11, all 75–550 pages, on white or cream with black ink, or
premium colour. No groundwood, no standard colour, no custom size.

## KDP: margins and bleed

| Page count | Inside margin | Outside, top and bottom, no bleed | With bleed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24–150 | 0.375 in | at least 0.25 in | at least 0.375 in |
| 151–300 | 0.5 in | at least 0.25 in | at least 0.375 in |
| 301–500 | 0.625 in | at least 0.25 in | at least 0.375 in |
| 501–700 | 0.75 in | at least 0.25 in | at least 0.375 in |
| 701–828 | 0.875 in | at least 0.25 in | at least 0.375 in |

KDP gives this table for paperback. No KDP page gives one for hardcover, so Press uses it for both until one does.

The table is the least KDP allows, not a design. Left to the page count, Press's inside margin is the table's figure and
0.375 in more: 0.75 in up to 150 pages, then 0.875, 1, 1.125 and 1.25. An author can set their own, and the check still holds
it to the table. Press's other margins are 0.625 in outside, 0.8 in at the top and 0.9 in at the bottom.

Bleed is 0.125 in on the top, bottom and outside edges: the page is 0.125 in wider and 0.25 in taller than the trim. If any
page bleeds, the whole interior is set up with bleed, and it has to be a PDF.

## KDP: the file

Source: [Paperback submission guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201857950).

- Single pages, not spreads; every page the same way up.
- Every font embedded; transparency and layers flattened.
- Pictures at least 300 dpi (600 at most is recommended); text at least 7 pt; lines at least 0.75 pt; grey fills at least 10%.
- No crop marks, bookmarks, comments, annotations, hidden objects, placeholder text, or locked or encrypted files.
- Page numbers in order, even on the left and odd on the right; roman numerals allowed in front matter.
- No missing or blank pages, beyond those a book's layout needs.
- At most 650 MB.

## KDP: covers

Source: [Create a paperback cover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201953020),
[Create a hardcover cover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GDTKFJPNQCBTMRV6) and
[Barcodes](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G5HDYGP4BXLX4RUW).

**Paperback width** = bleed + back + spine + front + bleed; **height** = bleed + trim height + bleed; bleed is 0.125 in.

| Paper | Spine width per page |
|---|---|
| White, black ink | 0.002252 in |
| Cream, black ink | 0.0025 in |
| Groundwood, black ink | 0.00235 in |
| Premium colour | 0.002347 in |
| Standard colour | 0.002252 in on the cover page; 0.002347 in on the submission page |

- Spine text only when the book has more than 79 pages (the cover page says both "at least 79" and "more than 79", so Press
  uses 80), at least 0.0625 in from each spine edge.
- Text at least 0.125 in inside the trim, and nothing from the front or back reaching into the spine.
- Barcode space: 2 × 1.2 in suggested, 1.4 × 0.8 in at least, at least 0.25 in from the spine and the trim, on solid white.
  If no barcode is supplied, KDP places one in the lower right of the back cover.
- One PDF: back, spine and front together. Images CMYK, no spot colours. 40 MB or less is recommended.

**Hardcover**: the art reaches 0.51 in past the edge of the front cover for the wrap; text and images at least 0.635 in from
the edge; a 0.4 in hinge between the spine and each cover's safe area, with no text or barcode in it; an own barcode at
least 0.76 in from the bottom. KDP's hardcover page gives no spine width per page; it's read from KDP's cover calculator
before v1.4 is built.

**Still unsettled at the source:** the standard-colour spine figure, the 79 or 80 page spine-text line, and a barcode
distance from the spine given as 6.3 mm on one page and 6 mm on another. Press takes the stricter figure each time.

## House rules

The owner's rules, taken from how his books are built. They're a preset: on for him, offered to everyone, each one a
setting.

| Rule | Setting |
|---|---|
| Body text 13 pt (13 to 14 for readability) | Body size |
| Every title in the contents starts on a right-hand page | Titles on the right |
| End on a blank page, with an even page count | End on a blank even page |
| No links in print | Links in print: off |
| Keep the published page count on a re-upload, so the cover still fits | Pin page count |
| No sentence split across a page turn, except the ones kept for suspense | Page turns, with a keep list |
| Every full page ends on the same bottom line | Even page bottoms |
| A dash never starts a line | Dash stays with the word before it |
| Curly quotes everywhere, and every quote paired | Quote check |
| No character falls back to another font | Missing-glyph check (stops the build) |
| Page numbers on the outside edge | Folio position |
| A page frame stays clear of the spine | Frame clearance (0.625 in by default) |
