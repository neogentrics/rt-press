# Your book and your privacy

Your book and your settings are kept in an encrypted project in RT Press's own folder, with a key of its own. With no password set, any copy of RT Press can open it, so that is privacy from a casual look, not secrecy.

Your words never leave your computer. RT Press has no account and sends nothing about you or your book.

## What goes online

- **Looking for a new version** asks GitHub whether a newer release exists. Nothing from your book goes with the question. You can turn it off in [Preferences](#do=preferences).
- **Links** in the About window and these guides open in your web browser.

The tutorial's practice book is a project of its own, made fresh and thrown away when you leave it. It never touches your book.

An `.rtbook` file from RT Story Tracker holds your book unencrypted, so RT Press deletes it once the book is safely in its own project. Your Word, Markdown and EPUB files are only read.
