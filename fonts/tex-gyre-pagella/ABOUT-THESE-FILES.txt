TeX Gyre Pagella, version 2.501, as it ships with RT Press.

These are four files of the TeX Gyre Pagella work, copied from CTAN on 28 November 2026 and not changed in any way:
texgyrepagella-regular.otf, texgyrepagella-italic.otf, texgyrepagella-bold.otf and texgyrepagella-bolditalic.otf.

This is only part of the work. The complete work, unmodified, with all the files its manifest lists, is at
https://ctan.org/pkg/tex-gyre-pagella and https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre

The work is by B. Jackowski, J. M. Nowacki and others, on behalf of TeX Users Groups. It is distributed under the GUST Font
